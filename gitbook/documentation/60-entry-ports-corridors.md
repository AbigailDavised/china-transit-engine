# 60 Open Entry Ports & Cross-Provincial Corridors

The NIA December 2025 expansion unified earlier 72-hour and 144-hour transit schemes into a **60-port, 24-province inter-provincial network**. Travelers admitted under 240-hour TWOV may now arrive at one approved port and depart from a different approved port across connected provincial stay areas.

## Port Composition (`2026.1` Dataset)

Across the 60 designated entry checkpoints validated in `china-transit-engine`:

- **47 International Airports** — Major hubs including Beijing Capital (`PEK`), Beijing Daxing (`PKX`), Shanghai Pudong (`PVG`), Shanghai Hongqiao (`SHA`), Guangzhou Baiyun (`CAN`), Shenzhen Bao'an (`SZX`), Chengdu Tianfu (`TFU`), Hangzhou Xiaoshan (`HGH`), Xi'an Xianyang (`XIY`), Chongqing Jiangbei (`CKG`), Xiamen Gaoqi (`XMN`), Kunming Changshui (`KMG`), Wuhan Tianhe (`WUH`), Qingdao Jiaodong (`TAO`), and Sanya Phoenix (`SYX`).
- **10 International Seaports** — Serving cruise and ferry arrivals across Shanghai, Tianjin, Qinhuangdao, Dalian, Lianyungang, Wenzhou, Zhoushan, Xiamen, Guangzhou, Shenzhen Shekou, and Beihai/Haikou.
- **3 Cross-Border Railway Ports** — Including the Mohan railway port corridor connecting Laos–Yunnan rail transit routes.

## Open-Jaw Cross-Provincial Travel Rules

Under the unified 240-hour policy:

1. **Nationwide Open-Jaw Eligibility**: Travelers holding confirmed onward international tickets from any of the 60 designated exit ports may travel overland (high-speed rail or domestic flights) between approved provincial stay zones across 24 provinces/municipalities, subject to border officer inspection at entry.
2. **Legacy Single-Corridor Checks**: Certain regional ports (such as Pearl River Delta / Guangdong ports or Yangtze River Delta clusters: Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui) retain specific local reporting requirements (including hotel/accommodation registration within 24 hours of check-in).
3. **High-Speed Rail & Hotel Registration Tip**: Foreign passport holders staying at hotels receive automatic police registration at reception; travelers staying with family or private apartments must register locally within 24 hours.
