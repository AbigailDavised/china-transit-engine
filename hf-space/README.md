# China 240-Hour Transit Without Visa (TWOV) Calculator

A zero-dependency, browser-native compliance calculator for international travelers transiting through Mainland China under the **240-Hour (10-Day) TWOV**, **15-Day Unilateral Visa-Free**, and **30-Day Bilateral Mutual Exemption** frameworks.

### Key Capabilities
- **Official NIA 00:00 Midnight Clock**: Computes exact statutory stay deadline starting from `00:00` on the day following entry (using pure calendar/CST math).
- **Third-Country Rule & Roundtrip Trap Detector**: Catches `A -> China -> A` roundtrip mistakes and recognizes separate customs territories (Hong Kong SAR, Macau SAR, Taiwan).
- **60 Open Ports & Open-Jaw Corridor Validation**: Covers all 60 eligible air, sea, and rail ports and validates regional cross-port exit corridors.
